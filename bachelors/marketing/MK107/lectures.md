
# MK107: Strategic Marketing Management — Global Campaigns & Analytics
## Bachelor of Science in Marketing — University of Yggdrasil, 2040

**Credits:** 4
**Description:** This course develops strategic planning skills for global marketing campaigns, integrating AI‑driven analytics, cross‑cultural brand narratives, and sustainable market positioning.
