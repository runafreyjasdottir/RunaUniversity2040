ANGOLA HISTORY & CULTURE
================================================================================

Founding of Angola, Indiana:
- Founded on June 28, 1838, by Thomas Gale and Cornelius Gilmore
- Angola post office established same year and has been in continuous operation since
- Named after Angola, New York

Potawatomi History:
- The Potawatomi people were the original inhabitants of this region
- In 1838, over 850 Potawatomi people were forced to march over 600 miles from Northcentral Indiana to eastern Kansas in the "Trail of Death"
- This forced removal occurred under government escort

Steuben County Geography:
- Steuben County has 105 lakes including Lake James, Lake George, Clear Lake, Jimmerson Lake, Lake Gage, Crooked Lake
- Pokagon State Park covers 1,260 acres with Potawatomi Nature Preserve and Beechwood Nature Preserve
- Pokagon State Park is situated on Lake James
